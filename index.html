Recruitment filter logic
  • Remove every player already in the current RR squad from acquisition targets.
  • Remove players marked unavailable because of a confirmed trade or existing franchise commitment.
  • Apply user-defined hard exclusions across every active shortlist.
  • Keep capped players out of uncapped-only lists.
  • Classify each candidate using the latest available squad, trade, international-cap and auction information; keep unconfirmed releases labelled as estimates.
  • Keep excluded names only in the separate exclusion register and research findings where relevant.
